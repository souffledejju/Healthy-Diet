# Healthy-Diet - AI-Powered Nutrition & Meal Planner 🥗🤖

Healthy-Diet is an intelligent web-based platform designed to assist users in achieving their ideal body weight and adopting healthier eating habits. Leveraging artificial intelligence (AI), the platform provides personalized dietary guidance, Body Mass Index (BMI) monitoring, daily meal planning, and comprehensive macronutrient tracking.

This collaborative project was developed as a proactive contribution toward **SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being**.

---

## 📌 Problem Background

Maintaining a balanced diet is a persistent everyday challenge for many:
* **Difficulty in Structuring a Healthy Diet:** People frequently struggle to find balanced meal combinations tailored to their bodily needs without direct access to a personal nutritionist.
* **Inconsistent Weight Management:** Without measurable tracking, efforts to lose or gain weight often lack consistency.
* **Team's Personal Motivation:** The inspiration stemmed directly from the team's lived experience, where 2 out of 3 group members had a BMI above the healthy threshold—sparking the urgency to build a practical, accessible diet companion.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Developing an all-in-one **AI Meal Planner** that helps users intelligently plan daily meals, whether their goal is weight loss, healthy weight gain, or sustaining long-term healthy habits.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **AI Meal Planner & Personalized Assistant:**
  Automated daily meal recommendations tailored to user profiles, health targets, and dietary preferences via AI.

* **BMI Calculator & Weight Goal Setting:**
  Accurately calculates Body Mass Index (BMI) based on current weight and visualizes the roadmap toward the ideal target weight.

* **Daily Calorie & Progress Tracker:**
  Monitors daily caloric intake and tracks bodily progress over time via dedicated profile and progress pages.

* **Curated Healthy Recipe Catalog:**
  Nutritious recipes with detailed cooking time estimates and full macronutrient breakdowns (protein, carbohydrates, fats, and total calories).

* **AI Chatbot & User Authentication:**
  Interactive virtual assistant for dietary inquiries alongside a secure user authentication system to persist personalized settings and history.
